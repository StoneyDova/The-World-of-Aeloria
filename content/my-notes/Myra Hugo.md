Orphaned as a child in the ever advancing streets of [[Elarian Spire]]. She was taken in by the Rohb Hugo who saw thought she may prove useful in his research of Ulcromite but mostly out of curiosity. She grew up in The Spire, the massive shining tower at the center of Elarian Spire.

Myra grew up in the upper levels of The Spire, so high up when she looked down rather than seeing the city she would see the tops of the clouds. Rohb hugo showed her many wonderful and fantastical creations, her whole life surrounded by magical and scientific advancements, sparking an interest in both, leading to her eventually becoming a Artificer. The Grung wizard found her intellectually interesting and showed her kindness, but did not understand children or their needs. He built magical constructs, Ulcromite powered automatons and unseen servants. Her favorite of these is her trusty companion, Ward, who was created to help her reach higher shelves in the massive library she frequented. The two would become close friends as he was slowly built up from step stool to fully sentient construct who never left her side.

As she grew older and more valuable to Rohb, she would soon take on the role of field research after expressing her desire to see outside of the spire. In the early days she would study just the caverns below the city, but soon she was sent all over the world on various missions or assignments. From Aureon and their magical Skylands to the barren and hostile Great ego where she was one of the only outsiders to ever gain the trust of the local tribe of agorians that called the sand their land, showing her their intricate tunnel system under the sands.

She is just returning from a trip to this desert land with research notes and a report ready to deliver to Rohb and head out on her next grand adventure. As she enters the city through the massive front gates, a large commotion rolls through the city.
