In only the distant past, the world of Aeloria was nothing more than a a swirling mass of cosmic magic and matter. The only form of life said to have existed during this time were the [[Mikrotherins]], a magic creature said to have existed longer than the universe. Soon the vast amounts of magic drew the attention of [[The Divine Ones]], 12 powerful gods who had destroyed their original patron planet in long unending wars waged between the gods and the mortals they had created to worship them. After the fall of their home world, they were left adrift in space, all wounded from the intense final clash that split their world at the atomic level. 

As [[The Divine Shapers]] drifted they were naturally drawn to the [[Mikrotherins]] and their powerful magic. Even 1000's years before they would ever see the chaos that would eventually become Aeloria they could feel its magic, pulling them closer and closer.

Soon they came upon the swirling mass and for the first time in their existence they all worked together, combining the last of their magic to calm the chaos and formed it into what we now know as Aeloria 

As they hurled towards the newly formed planet, the last of their consciousness, but really their deep distrust of each other, guided them towards [[The 13 continents]], 12 named after their respective gods, as they all fell into a deep slumber. The 13th continent was named [[Elaria]], said to named for the true god of this world, as well as their resting place, killed by the new intruder gods in a massive battle after most of them had awoken and regained their strength that left the land of [[Elaria]] barren and unlivable for 1000s of years with a massive crater at its center where the battle had taken place.

As life on the planet sprang forth [[The Divine Ones]] began to stir until eventually the first to wake was [[Aureon]], declaring himself and domain as the God of Law and the Skies, taking on the form of a massive gold 3 headed dragon. Soon his wife and only ally in [[The Old War]], [[Morvana]] rose from her slumber, proclaiming herself Goddess of Knowledge and Regret. Many say she took the form of a sleek black dragon with 9 heads and 3 tails. After reuniting with each other, the pair rose through the sky high above the clouds in a horribly beautiful dance of power, never to fall back down to the earth as they ruled from the skies. It is unknown where or how they have been able to stay so high in the air even after many millennium. 

Soon the Twin Gods of Storms and Sea,  [[Valerax]] and [[Lysanor]] respectively began to stir, causing massive storms and typhoons soon after when they fully awoke and began a 100 year wresting match that ended in a draw. The waves they caused were so massive and large it left the early aquatic life scattered and displaced all over the planet with many species dying after ending up on dry land. The ones who survived and adapted are the ancestors to all humanoid races on earth. As the brothers ended their cataclysmic horseplay they each came to settle where they had landed, two neighboring continents only separated by a small ocean.
