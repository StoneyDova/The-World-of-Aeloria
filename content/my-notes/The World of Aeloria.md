In only the distant past, the world of Aeloria was nothing more than a a swirling mass of cosmic magic and matter. The only form of life said to have existed during this time were the [[Mikrotherins]]. Soon the vast amounts of magic drew the attention of [[The Divine Shapers]], 13 powerful gods who had destroyed their original patreon planet in long unending wars between the gods and the mortals they had created to worship them. After the fall of their homeworld, they were left adrift in space, all wounded from the intense final clash that split their world at the atomic level. 

As [[The Divine Shapers]] drifted they were naturally drawn to the [[Mikrotherins]] and their powerful magic. Even 1000's years before they would ever see the chaos that would eventually become Aeloria they could feel its magic, pulling them closer and closer.
