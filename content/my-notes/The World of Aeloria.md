In only the distant past, the world of Aeloria was nothing more than a a swirling mass of cosmic magic and matter. The only form of life said to have existed during this time were the [[Mikrotherins]]. Soon the vast amounts of magic drew the attention of [[The Divine Ones]], 13 powerful gods who had destroyed their original patreon planet in long unending wars between the gods and the mortals they had created to worship them. After the fall of their homeworld, they were left adrift in space, all wounded from the intense final clash that split their world at the atomic level. 

As [[The Divine Shapers]] drifted they were naturally drawn to the [[Mikrotherins]] and their powerful magic. Even 1000's years before they would ever see the chaos that would eventually become Aeloria they could feel its magic, pulling them closer and closer.

Soon they came upon the swirling mass and for the first time in their existence they all worked together, combining the last of their magic to calm the chaos and formed it into what we now know as Aeloria 

As they hurled towards the newly formed planet, the last of their consiousness, but really their deep distrust of each other, guided them towards [[The 13 continents]], each named after their respective gods, as they all fell into a deep slumber. 

As life on the planet sprang forth [[The Divine Ones]] began to stir until eventually the first to wake was [[Aureon]], declaring himself and domain as the God of Law and the Skies, taking on the form of a massive gold 3 headed dragon.
