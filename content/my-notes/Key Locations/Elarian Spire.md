Elarian Spire was once nothing more than peaceful and remote farmland. After the discovery of [[Ulcromite]] the city was quickly built up into a bustling 500 square mile metropolis. The grung wizard [[Rohb Hugo]] was instrumental in its rise as well as the rise of the current royal family, [[House Alari]] and the current king [[King Ortho Alari]] . He achieved this and much more through a tightly kept secret method that allowed him imbue Ulcromite with magic in a variety of ways that in combination with inventions from members of [[The Order]]. The city rose and expanded quickly attracting the attention of every kingdom as well as a much darker gaze.

### **Congruent Timeline of Elarian Spire & Key Characters**

**c. 55 Years Before Present (BP)**

- **The Discovery:** Farmers in the **Elarian Basin** plow up strange, silvery stones from the deep **[[Ulcromite Caverns]]**. They are considered simple curiosities, used as decorations and paperweights.
    

**c. 53 BP – Rohb's Revelation**

- **The Breakthrough:** The Grung wizard **[[Rohb Hugo]]**, one of many mages drawn by rumors of the stones, makes a singular discovery. While others conclude the stones are inert, he alone learns how to successfully imbue **[[Ulcromite]]** with magic, proving it can store and release vast amounts of energy. This is the spark.
    

**c. 50 BP – The Land Rush & The Founding**

- **The Gold Rush:** News of Rohb's discovery leaks, causing a frenzy. Prospectors, merchants, and kingdoms offer fortunes for the seemingly worthless farmland.
    
- **The Five Families Refuse:** Amidst the pressure, five farming families, including the gnomish farmer and secret member of [[The Order]], **[[Ortho Alari]]** (owner of the largest plot), the stubborn **[[House Hanada]]**, [[House Stoneborn]], [[House Mithra]] and [[House Oduri]] refuse to sell their ancestral holdings.
    
- **The Pact of Five:** These families form a council. Leveraging Alari's genius, Rohb's knowledge, and the collective land of all the houses, they break ground on a new city, first establishing **Smoke Alley** to begin systematic Ulcromite processing.
    

**c. 45-40 BP – The First Boom**

- **Smoke Alley Expands:** A vast, polluted district of factories and smelters emerges, its forges and engines powered by Ulcromite cores. The constant noise of industry becomes the city's heartbeat.
    
- **The Foundry Ward is Carved Out:** As metalworking and Ulcromite-core forging become specialized trades, a dedicated sub-district within Smoke Alley is established, known for its intense heat and master artisans.
    
- **The Grand Bazaar Rises:** A sprawling commercial hub of markets, inns, and trade houses springs up to support the booming population, quickly becoming a hotspot for legitimate trade and illicit smuggling.
    
- **The Docks District is Established:** To handle the influx of materials and goods, the **Sundown Docks** and larger harbor district are constructed along a newly engineered canal system, quickly falling under the influence of [[House Hanada]].
    
- **The Garrison is Founded:** A dedicated military district is established for barracks, training grounds, and the development of new Ulcromite-powered war machines, solidifying the city's defensive might.
    
- **The Pit Forms:** In the city's shadow, a sprawling shantytown of cheap labor and desperate poor takes root, festering into a crime-ridden slum rarely touched by the law. The area known as the **[[Low Spire]]** becomes its most notorious sector.
    
- **The Ashfields are Claimed:** [[House Hanada]] formally lays claim to the ashen, mineral-rich plots of land bordering their original farm, establishing their industrial heartland.
    

**c. 40-35 BP – The Siege & The Spire**

- **The Siege of Nyxdrogo:** The giant black elder dragon **[[Nyxdrogo]]**, drawn by the concentrated magical potential of the Ulcromite stores, attacks. She destroys nearly a third of the city's early structures.
    
- **The Hero & The Blade:** The dragon is slain by the warrior **[[Tarrik Stoneborn]]** wielding a rare, prototype **[[Ulcromite Blade]]**.
    
- **The Great Project Begins:** In the siege's aftermath, **[[King Ortho Alari]]** (newly crowned) and **[[Rohb Hugo]]** initiate the monumental project of building **The Spire**—a massive Ulcromite tower designed to generate a permanent protective dome.
    

**c. 30-25 BP – An Age of Security and Order**

- **The Dome is Raised:** The Ulcromite core of **The Spire** is activated, and the shimmering protective dome seals over the city for the first time. Elarian Spire becomes an unbreachable fortress.
    
- **The Spire District (Elarion's Crown) is Formalized:** The area surrounding the tower becomes the exclusive administrative and residential district for the government and the noble families.
    
- **The Moss Tower is Built:** To fuel the Spire's immense energy needs, the moss-covered prison-fortress of **Moss Tower** is constructed. Under **[[Warden Kestrel Raithos]]**, prisoners are put to work mining the Ulcromite Caverns below.
    
- **The Golden Clad is Formed:** The elite royal guard is established, their golden armor becoming a symbol of the new, secure era.
    
- **The Garden District is Developed:** The affluent and the noble families carve out a clean, green residential area from the last remnants of the old farmland, a stark contrast to the industrial and lower districts.
    
- **The Temple District Rises:** As the city's population swells with immigrants from across the world, a district dedicated to old gods, new cults, and spiritual resistance to the tide of technology organically takes shape.
    

**c. 23 BP – A New Generation**

- **Rion Hanada is Born:** The second son of **[[House Hanada]]** is born, his crimson eyes a clear mark of the family's fiery, otherworldly heritage.
    

**c. 22 BP – An Orphan in the Spire**

- **Myra is Orphaned:** A young girl named **[[Myra]]** is left orphaned on the ever-advancing streets of the newly secured city.
    
- **A New Home:** **[[Rohb Hugo]]**, likely out of curiosity, discovers Myra and takes her in. He raises her in the sterile, wondrous environment of the upper levels of **The Spire** itself, building her a companion, **Ward**, who evolves from a step-stool into a sentient construct.
    

**c. 20 BP – A Center for Learning**

- **The Scholar's Enclave is Founded:** **[[Rohb Hugo]]** establishes a formal district for universities, libraries, and research laboratories, attracting the brightest minds to study Ulcromite applications and other advanced sciences.
    

**c. 15-10 BP – Childhood and Training**

- **Myra's Education:** Myra grows up surrounded by magical and scientific advancements, her intellect flourishing under Rohb's detached but benevolent tutelage. She becomes a skilled Artificer, her life a blend of magic and machine.
    
- **Rion's Upbringing:** **[[Rion Hanada]]** grows up learning the dual nature of his house. He is a noble scion in silk robes in **The Spire**, and a brawler in the soot-covered fighting pits of his family's **[[Ash League]]**, learning to channel the violet flame in his blood.
    

**c. 10 BP – High-Security Research**

- **The Aetherium is Created:** A high-security zone within the Scholar's Enclave is cordoned off for **[[Rohb Hugo]]**'s most dangerous and classified Ulcromite experiments, rumored to involve live subjects.
    
- **[[Dagger & Gavel]]** form a private investigation firm located in [[The Pit]] after growing disillusioned with the current law systems in The Spire and become a constant thorn in the ruling classes side.
    

**c. 7 BP – A Grove in the North**

- **Osryn's Grove is Founded:** The Dragonborn druid **[[Osryn Marowar]]** establishes a safe haven in the **[[Giant Vords]]** forest. For five years, his grove grows, a peaceful sanctuary in a lawless land.
    

**c. 4 BP – Myra Sees the World**

- **Myra Becomes a Field Researcher:** Now 18, **[[Myra Hugo]]** expresses her desire to see the world beyond the Spire. She becomes Rohb's field agent, traveling from the skylands of **[[Aureon]]** to the barren **[[Great Ego]]**, where she earns the trust of the native Agorians.
    

**c. 3 BP – A Year of Tragedy and Mission**

- **The Grove is Destroyed:** The dark elf witch **[[Vordaiya]]**, leading an army of cultists and undead, sacks Osryn's grove. She murders his wife and child and curses him, using a powerful blast of magic to rend the flesh from his bones, leaving him a conscious skeleton.
    
- **Edrin Cloud's Mission Begins:** On the continent of **[[Aureon]]**, the secret son of **[[House Cloud]]**, **[[Edrin]]**, is discovered by the **[[Mad Queen Darysis]]**. To atone for his parents' deceit and save their lives, he is sent to Elaria as a spy to steal samples of raw and powered Ulcromite. He is given a five-year deadline.
    

**c. 2 BP – Arrivals and Captures**

- **Osryn Arrives:** After six months of searching the forest and a year of fruitless investigation in the city, the skeletal **[[Osryn Marowar]]** partners with the private investigators of **[[Dagger & Gavel]]**, trading his services for their help in finding Vordaiya.
    
- **Edrin is Captured:** **[[Edrin Cloud]]** is captured shortly after arriving in Elaria and imprisoned in **[[The Moss Tower]]**. For three years, he is a subject of brutal Ulcromite experiments, one of which replaces his arm with a mechanical prosthesis powered by a small Ulcromite core—removing it would kill him.
    

**PRESENT DAY (Year 0) – Converging Fates**

- **Myra's Return (Age 21):** **[[Myra Hugo]]** returns to the city through the massive front gates, research notes from the Great Ego in hand, ready to report to Rohb. As she enters, a large commotion rolls through the city.
    
- **Rion's Ascent (Age 21):** **[[Rion Hanada]]**, the "Violet Knuckle," is deep in his quiet war—publicly pushing for legitimacy while secretly tightening his grip on the underworld from the **[[Sundown Docks]]**. His ambition burns hotter than ever.
    
- **Edrin's Escape:** After three long years of imprisonment and honing his skills, **[[Edrin Cloud]]** executes his escape from **[[The Moss Tower]]**. His desperate breakout is the direct cause of the city-wide commotion that greets Myra. He is now a fugitive, determined to find Ulcromite and return home within two years, even if it means tearing the core from his own arm.
    
- **Osryn's Hunt:** **[[Osryn Marowar]]** continues his stalled quest for vengeance, working cases for [[Dagger & Gavel]]. The chaos of the prison break presents new opportunities and dangers, potentially shaking loose the very information he seeks.
