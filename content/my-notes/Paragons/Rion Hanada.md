Rion Hanada, known in the shadowed fighting pits under the [[Low Spire]] as the [[Violet Knuckle]], is the second son of the lower noble [[House Hanada]]. His family, one of [[The Five Families]], clawed its way up from the dirt of the [[Elarian Basin]], and that gritty origin is a truth Rion carries in his bones and the fire in his blood. The stories say their founder, [[Rokuro Hanada]], married a fire spirit, and the pact left a permanent mark. For Rion, this is not some old tale; it's the violet flame that writhes just under his skin, flaring in his eyes when he's angry and erupting across his knuckles in the heat of a fight.

While his house officially holds the [[Ashfields]] and runs the smelters that feed the city's forges, their true power has always been in the underworld. They are nobles in silk robes who never stopped being the most formidable gang in the [[Sundown Docks]], controlling the black-market trade of volatile [[Ulcromite residue]] and the brutal, bare-knuckle boxing rings of the [[Ash League]]. Rion is the sharpest expression of this duality. To the nobles in [[The Spire]], he is a charming, if somewhat crude, scion of a disreputable house. To the fighters and enforcers of the [[Ember Sons]]—his family's personal army of street brawlers—he is their prince, the [[Violet Knuckle]] who fights alongside them, his bare fists leaving searing brands on anything they touch.

He is currently waging a quiet war on two fronts. Publicly, he's begun a campaign to legitimize the family, pushing for clean sponsorships and official contracts, trying to wash the soot of the docks from their name. But in the shadows, he's tightening his grip on their illicit operations, knowing that the power to negotiate comes from a position of unshakable strength. This double life is a precarious balance, and many in the city watch, waiting for the moment his ambition burns too bright. They whisper that a man who is half-fire cannot help but eventually set his own world ablaze, and Rion Hanada burns hotter than any of them.
