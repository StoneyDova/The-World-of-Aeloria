**c. 1,000,000 BE (Before Elaria)**  
In the distant past, the world of Aeloria was nothing more than a swirling mass of cosmic magic and matter. The only form of life said to have existed during this time were the [[Mikrotherins]], magical creatures said to have existed longer than the universe.

**c. 750,000 BE**  
Soon the vast amounts of magic drew the attention of [[The Divine Ones]], 12 powerful gods who had destroyed their original patron planet in long, unending wars waged between the gods and the mortals they had created to worship them. After the fall of their home world, they were left adrift in space, all wounded from the intense final clash that split their world at the atomic level.

**c. 500,000 BE**  
As [[The Divine Shapers]] drifted, they were naturally drawn to the [[Mikrotherins]] and their powerful magic. Even thousands of years before they would ever see the chaos that would eventually become Aeloria, they could feel its magic, pulling them closer and closer.

**c. 10,000 BE (The Shaping)**  
Soon they came upon the swirling mass and for the first time in their existence, they all worked together, combining the last of their magic to calm the chaos and form it into what we now know as Aeloria.

**c. 9,900 BE (The Descent)**  
As they hurtled towards the newly formed planet, the last of their consciousness, but really their deep distrust of each other, guided them towards [[The 13 continents]], 12 named after their respective gods, as they all fell into a deep slumber. The 13th continent was named [[Elaria]], said to be named for the true god of this world, as well as their resting place, killed by the new intruder gods in a massive battle after most of them had awoken and regained their strength—a battle that left the land of [[Elaria]] barren and unlivable for thousands of years with a massive crater at its center where the battle had taken place.

**c. 8,500 BE (The First Awakening)**  
As life on the planet sprang forth, [[The Divine Ones]] began to stir until eventually the first to wake was [[Aureon]], declaring himself and his domain as the God of peace and the Order, taking on the form of a massive gold three-headed dragon. Soon his wife and only ally in [[The Old War]], [[Morvana]], rose from her slumber, proclaiming herself Goddess of Knowledge. Many say she took the form of a sleek black dragon with nine heads and three tails. After reuniting with each other, the pair rose through the sky high above the clouds in a horribly beautiful dance of power, never to fall back down to the earth as they ruled from the skies. It is unknown where or how they have been able to stay so high in the air even after many millennia.

The magic that drew the panteon to the planet had not been consumed in the birth of the world, but transformed and transferred.

**c. 8,480 BE (The Harbinger)**  
Aetheria felt a special connection to this energy and became the harbinger of the innate arcana in the world. She would take many forms as the centuries went on, traveling the realm as she would bless many artifacts create many magic items by molding the energy of the world.

**c. 8,200 BE (The Second Awakening)**  
Soon the Twin Gods of storm and Sea, [[Valerax]] and [[Lysanor]] respectively, began to stir, causing massive storms and typhoons soon after they fully awoke and began a 100-year wrestling match that ended in a draw. The waves they caused were so massive and large it left the early aquatic life scattered and displaced all over the planet, with many species dying after ending up on dry land. The ones who survived and adapted are the ancestors to all humanoid races on Aeloria. As the brothers ended their cataclysmic horseplay, they each came to settle where they had landed, two neighboring continents only separated by a small ocean. They would share the domain of tempest.

**c. 8,100 BE (The Third Awakening)**  
Next rose the nature god [[Vorda]]. Seeing the barren rocky land crawling with former sea life, the land still healing from the trauma of birth, displeased him. He remembered the plentiful plant life from his former home and wept at what he had lost. In a cry of anguish, a powerful blast erupted from the god, lush greenery left in its wake. This blast permeated the entire world, transforming the harsh environment into a bold and diverse ecosystem. The freshly displaced life from the sea thrived in these new conditions and evolution quickly took hold. The powerful magic that was once a swirling chaos now lay at the core of the planet, its effects speeding up the evolution process. After this immense display of power and passion, Vorda fell to the ground, now soft with the petals of thousands of vibrant flowers. He fell into a deep slumber right on the spot and to this day remains there. Hundreds of temples and buildings were raised in the area to hide the location of the sleeping god, with a group of devout priests acting as his eternal bodyguards until the day he rises once again in the land named after him.

**c. 8,100 BE (The Sundering of Light)**  
As [[Aurelian]] fell to the planet after its creation, they split into two aspects: [[Haelian]] and [[Nymera]], light and twilight. Each would fall on opposite sides of the new planet and each would wake at exactly the same moment following [[Vorda]]'s guttural scream. They would each take the form of large direwolves, Haelian golden and luminecant, Nymera silver and glowing. They longed to be with one another, two sides of the same coin, chasing each other at but one never able to outpace the other, acting as an endless cycle. Many say the celestial bodies of the sun and moon each have a favored god to catch up to the other first and follow this pursuit closely.

**c. 7,500 BE (The Gift of Life)**  
As life sprang forth on the planet, the life god [[Anathis]] stirred from her slumber, feeling its desire to survive. While the species had evolved and grown since Vorda had given them a place to do so, they remained soulless animals and monsters, none capable of true growth on an individual level. She took the form of a Giant Sky Whale, silvery and incandescent with hundreds of flipper-like wings and a long, flowy tail that spanned miles. She rose through the sky and began to travel around the entire world, leaving a sparkling dust in her trail that slowly drifted down to the earth. All the creatures the world over were covered in the dust as it also filled their lungs. These were the first generation of true life on Aeloria, with all their descendants being born with the gift of Anathis.

**c. 7,490 BE (The Mercy of Death)**  
It would not be long before death began to stir, [[Pyralis]], the goddess of death and grave. The sister of the life goddess, she existed as a check on her older sister's reckless gift-giving. The mortals would come to fear and despise her but knew not the curse that had been laid upon them. When Anathis granted souls to mortals, she gave no thought to the ravages of time and aging, which could not be halted. Eventually, all of the life on the newly formed planet would either fall to an injury they could never rise from but be denied the release of death, or devolve into shriveled husks, unable to move or breathe as their aged skin fell from their bones. Anathis cared not for this and only cared to see life and all its twists and turns as a spectator. Pyralis knew that this would happen, just as it had happened when they came into existence on their home world. Only this time, she would not watch for generations as her sister's playthings came to understand the truth of what had been given to them. She took the form of a sleek black jaguar, no larger than any other. She weaved through the land with incredible speed, crossing oceans and continents in the blink of an eye, leaving a black trail of smoke that billowed out and filled the lungs of all the surrounding life, leaving them vulnerable but free to live and die as they please.

**c. 5,000 BE (The First Apprentice)**  
Soon the forge god [[Ingenium]] would rise, taking a shape future sentinels would be modeled after. Caring not for mortals or the land they now called home, Ingenium would retreat deep into the planets core where he built his [[All craft]]. There in his forge he would tinker and craft endlessly, taking joy from all the novel new metals and alloys that comprised his new home. Before long he would take on a mortal, [[Daemunodious]], teaching him all his secrets and techniques in exchange for his servitude. As the years wore on [[Daemunodious]] would grow weak and frail with age. Ingenium had grown to love the mortal and was bereaved at his immanent death. He had sought out the other gods and begged them to save the man, but all told him time could not be stopped. As he returned to the man, now on deaths door, he knelt by his side and wept. He told the man he would grant any wish in his power. As the man succumbed the the ravages of age, he only asked the god one thing: to teach his craft to mortals. Ingenium would honor this wish and soon had many apprentice in the ever expanding [[All craft]]. These mortals would soon take on their own apprentice and soon [[The Order]] was born, a secret society of blacksmiths and inventors dedicated to the many crafts gifted to them by [[Ingenium]].

**c. 1,200 BE (The Mortal Age Begins)**  
With advancements in mechanics and technology, the world was quickly transformed. Where there once stood quant villages and towns rose massive cities. As populations boomed and security became less scare, mortals began to a hunger for positions of power and authority over this new world. Politics and greed swept the 13 continents as governments and kingdoms began to stake their claim.

**c. 1,150 BE (The Shadow's Rise)**  
[[Lokkar]] the god of war and trickery had yet to stir all these years, yet this new influence of industry and greed was a storm he could not sleep through. He took the form of a simple human in simple black garb. He spent all his early days just surveying the mortals and the large cities they gathered at to hide from the ever present danger of the many monsters that still roamed the land. After many years acting as a passive observer, Lokkar began to influence the course of politics and gave heartfelt speeches on both sides of rebellions as they rose, never truly on anyone's side. He came to be known by many names in many kingdoms but it is said he still wanders the land clad in black, sowing chaos and misery wherever he goes.
