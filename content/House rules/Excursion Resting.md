

### Excursion & Bastion Resting

A character's ability to recover their strength and resources depends greatly on their environment, ranging from the dangers of the wilderness to the security of a fortified home.

#### Short Rest
A Short Rest is a period of downtime, at least 1 hour long, during which a character does nothing more strenuous than eating, drinking, reading, and tending to wounds. A character can spend one or more Hit Dice to heal at the end of a Short Rest.

*   **Anywhere:** A Short Rest can be attempted in any environment.
*   **Interrupted by:** The following activities will interrupt a Short Rest, requiring the character to start over:
    *   Rolling Initiative.
    *   Casting a spell with a spell slot other than a cantrip.
    *   Taking any damage.
    *   Engaging in more than 1 hour of strenuous activity (e.g., standing watch, crafting, digging).

#### Long Rest
A Long Rest is a period of extended downtime intended for full recovery. The requirements and benefits differ based on location.

**1. Excursion Resting (In the Wilderness or Dungeon)**
Achieving a full Long Rest outside of safety is a demanding process.
*   **Requirements:** A character must complete **three consecutive Short Rests** in the same secure location (e.g., a well-hidden campsite, a barricaded room) without any of the rests being interrupted.
*   **Benefits:** Upon completion, a character regains all spent Hit Points and Hit Dice. They also regain half their spent spell slots (rounded down) and any limited-use class features that reset on a Long Rest.
*   **Consequence of Interruption:** If any of the three Short Rests is interrupted, the entire Long Rest process fails. The character may have benefited from the individual Short Rests that were completed, but they do not gain the full Long Rest benefits.

**2. Bastion Resting (In a Safe Haven)**
Resting within the security of a Bastion—a character's home, a fortified keep, a friendly temple, or a trusted town—allows for true, uninterrupted recovery.
*   **Requirements:** A character must spend at least **6 hours of sleep** and **2 hours of light activity** in a single night within their Bastion or another designated Safe Haven.
*   **Benefits:** Upon completion, a character gains the full benefits of a Long Rest: all lost Hit Points and Hit Dice, all spent spell slots, and all limited-use class features are regained.
*   **Bastion Point Bonus:** If a character rests in a Bastion they own, they also regain one spent **Bastion Point** at the end of the rest, representing the morale and logistical boost of sleeping in one's own bed and managing one's holdings.
