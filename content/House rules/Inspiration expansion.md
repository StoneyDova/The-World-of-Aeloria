
### Heroic & Historic Inspiration

Inspiration is a rule that rewards players for engaging with the story and world. This system uses two distinct types: **Heroic Inspiration**, earned through character deeds, and **Historic Inspiration**, earned through world-shaping events.

#### Heroic Inspiration
This represents the spark of heroism that shines during personal moments of excellence.

*   **Function:** Functions identically to standard Inspiration. A player can have only one Heroic Inspiration at a time.
*   **Awarding:**
    *   **By the DM:** The DM can award it for exceptional roleplaying, clever problem-solving, or a character action that perfectly embodies their traits, ideals, bonds, or flaws.
    *   **Between Players:** At any time, a player can nominate another player to receive Heroic Inspiration for a moment of **good roleplay** (e.g., a poignant character moment, a witty in-character remark, or a selfless act that drives the story). The DM has final discretion to approve or deny the award.
*   **Usage:** When you have Heroic Inspiration, you can expend it to grant yourself **Advantage** on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

#### Historic Inspiration
This represents the weight and significance of a character's actions upon the world itself. It is a tangible fragment of destiny.

*   **Function:** A token awarded when a character's actions cause or bear witness to a truly historic event. A player can hold multiple Historic Inspiration tokens at once.
*   **Awarding:**
    *   **The DM** awards Historic Inspiration when a player or party causes an event that will be recorded in the world's history. Examples include: slaying a legendary beast that plagued a kingdom for centuries, brokering a peace treaty between two warring nations, discovering a lost city, or personally witnessing the fall of a god.
    *   It is given to the player(s) directly responsible, or to those who were pivotal witnesses.
*   **Usage:** A Historic Inspiration token is a **"coupon" that can be exchanged with the DM for one automatic d20 roll result.**
    *   When you expend a Historic Inspiration token, you can choose to **treat any single d20 roll as a natural 20.** This can be declared *after* seeing the roll, but before the DM announces the outcome.
    *   This cannot be used on a death saving throw, as fate is the sole arbiter of life and death.
