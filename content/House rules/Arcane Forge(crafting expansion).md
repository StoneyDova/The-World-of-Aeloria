

### **The Arcane Forge: A Masterwork Guide to Materials & Enchantments**

In the grand tapestry of adventure, the mightiest legends are often woven around artifacts of unparalleled power. These are not merely found; they are *forged*. They are the products of will, skill, and the rarest substances on the material plane. This is the domain of the Arcane Forge—a system that empowers you to become a shaper of legends, crafting the very weapons and armor needed to challenge the divine.

To build such marvels, one must first master the materials. What follows is a comprehensive guide to the essential components of creation: the mystical Essences that provide a catalyst for magic, and the legendary Metals that form an item's unyielding foundation.

---

#### **I. The Catalyst: Harnessing Essences**

An essence is a rare component that embodies the magic you wish to instill. It is the soul of the item, a physical manifestation of power harvested from the world's most dangerous creatures or discovered in forgotten vaults.

**The Purpose of an Essence:**
*   **Halved Crafting Cost:** Spending an appropriate essence during creation halves the gold cost of the item's raw materials.
*   **Simplified Sourcing:** Once an essence is used, any remaining material components are considered mundane and readily available.

**Harvesting Essences from Foes:**
The spoils of a hard-won battle can be more than just treasure. After defeating a monster, a character can:
1.  **Study the Creature:** Make an Arcana check (DC 10 + the creature's CR) to identify a harvestable essence and the required part (e.g., an Ankheg's acid gland).
2.  **Harvest the Essence:** If the creature died within the last round, make a Survival check (DC 10 + the creature's CR) to successfully extract the essence. Failure destroys it.

The creature's Challenge Rating guides the potency of the essence and the rarity of items it can help create:

| Creature CR | Essence Rarity |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 or higher | Uncommon |
| 5 or higher | Rare |
| 15 or higher | Very Rare |
| 20 or higher | Legendary |

**Crafting Requirements:**
The number of ingots required scales with the item's size and complexity:
*   **Light Weapon:** 1 Ingot
*   **Weapon or Light Armor:** 2 Ingots
*   **Heavy Weapon or Shield:** 3 Ingots
*   **Medium Armor:** 4 Ingots
*   **Heavy Armor:** 5 Ingots
*   **Plate Armor:** 6 Ingots

#### **III. The Art of Enchantment: A Framework for Power**

With the physical form complete, the weaving of magic begins. The system provides a vast menu of enchantments, categorized for clarity.

**1. Universal Enchantments:** Properties that can be applied to almost any item.
*   **Uncommon:** Darkvision, Glowing, Returning.
*   **Rare:** Ego (social expertise), Expertise (skill expertise), Resistance (to a damage type), Spell Resistance.
*   **Very Rare:** Lucky (reroll a failed d20 test).
*   **Legendary:** Invisibility, True Seeing.

**2. Weapon Enchantments:** Properties that define a weapon's combat role.
*   **Uncommon:** Binding Chains (grapple on hit), Cruel (gain THP on kill), Marking (track your prey), Throwing.
*   **Rare:** Cursing (psychic damage and penalty), Dueling (compels 1v1 combat), Flame Tongue (extra fire damage), Life Stealing (on a crit), Vicious (extra damage).
*   **Very Rare:** Dancing (self-operating weapon), Frost Brand (cold damage and utility), Mighty Cleaving (strike multiple foes), Sharpness (extra damage and exhaustion on a crit).
*   **Legendary:** Holy Avenger (vs. Fiends/Undead), Thunderbolt (stunning boom), Vorpal (decapitation).

**3. Armor Enchantments:** Properties that enhance a wearer's resilience and mobility.
*   **Uncommon:** Acrobat's Agility (Dex 19), Beastly Senses (Wis 19), Giant Endurance (Con 19), Instant Armor (don/doff as an action), Water Breathing.
*   **Rare:** Ambusher (blindsight, can't be surprised), Antimage (end a spell on you), Feathered Wings (fly speed), Fey Step (reaction teleport and invisibility), Speed (doubled movement).
*   **Very Rare:** Elusive (no opportunity attacks).
*   **Legendary:** Invulnerability (immunity to B/P/S damage).

**Key Enchantment Limitations:**
*   **Three Enchantments Maximum:** No item can hold more than three enchantments. Adding a fourth requires removing one.
*   **No Stacking:** The same enchantment cannot be applied multiple times.
*   **Attunement:** If any single property requires attunement, the entire item does.
