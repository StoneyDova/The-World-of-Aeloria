###  Paragon Abilities

A character’s subclass represents a focused path of study and a specialization of their core talents. A Paragon Ability is a feature gained upon reaching **11th level**, allowing a character to transcend the normal limits of their specialization. This ability forges a unique synergy between subclass features and other class features, creating a new, signature expression of their power that is uniquely their own[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Master's_Guide)[](https://wethenerdy.com/master-of-worlds-adventures-and-rules-a-look-at-the-5e-dms-guide/).

When you gain a Paragon Ability, work with your Dungeon Master to select an option that fits your character's story. These abilities are designed with three core principles in mind[](https://timbannock.com/dungeons-dragons-5th-edition-dungeon-masters-guide-review/):

- **Synergistic.** The ability creates a new, compelling interaction between your existing features.
    
- **Limited.** Its power is balanced by a reasonable resource cost, limitation, or trade-off.
    
- **Iconic.** It feels like a pinnacle expression of your character’s specific path.
